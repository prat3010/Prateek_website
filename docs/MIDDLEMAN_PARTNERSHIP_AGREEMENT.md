## 1. PURPOSE & ENGAGEMENT OVERVIEW
This Agreement outlines the commercial terms, commission structure, payment schedules, and operational protocols between **Prateeq Sharma** ("Developer") and **[Partner Name]** ("Sales Representative / Partner") for bringing client web development, custom software, and AI integration projects to the Developer.

---

## 2. ROLES & RESPONSIBILITIES

### **A. Sales Representative / Partner Responsibilities:**
1. **Lead Generation & Client Relationship:** Prospecting, initiating client contact, and acting as the primary business contact.
2. **Scoping Brief Collection:** Distributing the official **Client Discovery & Scoping Brief** (via `prateeq.in/?mode=business#intake` web form or printable A4 PDF brief) to collect client goals, features, asset readiness, and budget range.
3. **Quotation Delivery & Follow-up:** Delivering the formal fixed-price quotation (prepared by Developer) to the client and securing signed brief & initial deposit.

### **B. Developer / Lead Architect Responsibilities:**
1. **Technical Proposal & Scoping:** Reviewing filled briefs and preparing accurate, fixed-price proposals within 24 hours.
2. **Full-Stack Engineering & AI Development:** Architecting, coding, testing, and deploying custom Next.js, Supabase, and AI RAG platforms.
3. **Staging & Handoff:** Hosting client alpha/beta builds on developer staging environments, executing QA, and conducting final production domain deployment.
4. **Technical Alignment Calls:** Joining 15-minute technical discovery calls alongside Partner to address complex architecture, security, or API questions.

## 3. COMMISSION & COMPENSATION STRUCTURE

Commission is calculated as a percentage of the net contract value (excluding third-party infrastructure fees such as domain registration or direct client cloud costs). The selected band is set by the signed contract value of the project:

| Project Tier & Value Range | Commission Cut (%) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tier A** <br> *(₹1 – ₹75,000 / $1 – $1,000)* | **10%** |
| **Tier B** <br> *(₹75,001 – ₹2,49,999 / $1,001 – $3,299)* | **12%** |
| **Tier C** <br> *(₹2,50,000+ / $3,300+)* | **15%** |

### **Recurring Monthly Maintenance Commission:**
- For any client who subscribes to a **Monthly Care Plan** (e.g., ₹5,000/month or $70/month), Partner receives a **10% recurring monthly commission** for as long as the client retainer remains active.

---

## 4. PAYMENT DISBURSEMENT RULES

1. **No Out-of-Pocket Liability:** Developer will never pay commissions out-of-pocket prior to client funds clearing bank accounts.
2. **Proportional Disbursement Schedule:**
   - **50% of Total Commission:** Disbursed within 48 business hours of Developer receiving the Client's 50% Upfront Deposit.
   - **50% of Total Commission:** Disbursed after the Client's Final 50% Balance clears.
3. **Cancellations & Refunds:** In the event of a client default, scope cancellation, or partial payment, commission will be calculated strictly on net funds actually collected and retained by Developer.

---

## 5. TECHNICAL ALIGNMENT & REPRESENTATION PROTOCOL

1. **Positioning:** Partner represents Developer as the *Lead Technical Partner / Principal Solutions Architect*.
2. **Technical Alignment Calls:** If a prospective client asks technical, database, RLS security, or AI retrieval questions beyond Partner's expertise, Partner will schedule a 15-minute Technical Alignment Call with Developer.
3. **Scope Control:** Partner will not promise extra pages, features, or custom integrations without Developer's written approval. All extra feature requests mid-project will be quoted under a formal *Change Order*.

---

## 6. NON-CIRCUMVENTION & CONFIDENTIALITY

1. **Non-Circumvention:** Partner agrees not to bypass Developer or refer introduced clients to alternative software developers without Developer's express written consent.
2. **Client Ownership & IP:** All codebase assets, databases, and intellectual property remain the property of Developer until 100% of the project contract fees are paid by Client.
3. **Confidentiality:** Both parties agree to keep project quotes, client contact information, and internal commercial terms strictly confidential.
